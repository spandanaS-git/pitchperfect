## 2. Key Synthesis & Insights

### Insight 1: The "Resume vs. Argument" Mismatch
* Instructors currently send credential-heavy resumes listing 200-hour and 500-hour teacher training certificates.
* **Buyers rarely care about certificate hours.** 
  * Corporate buyers want **stress deltas** and **liability insurance**.
  * Studio owners want **retention rates** and **fill percentage**.
  * Schools want **background checks** and **group size handling**.

### Insight 2: The Data Exists, But It Is Fragmented
* Every instructor interviewed has data, but it is split:
  * Attendance lives in Mindbody, Momoyoga, or Square exports.
  * Student feedback lives in Google Forms or informal text messages.
  * Event outcomes live in Google Calendar notes and Venmo logs.
* PitchPerfect's 3-file model (`class_history`, `survey_responses`, `event_outcomes`) perfectly maps to these three distinct buckets.

### Insight 3: The Need for a "Gap Plan"
* Instructors noted that even when they don't have enough data (e.g., they have 0 corporate surveys), having a tool tell them **"You need 15 surveys over the next 3 weeks to unlock corporate wellness"** gives them an actionable next step rather than a generic rejection.

## 3. Taxonomy Validation
The feedback confirmed our 7 opportunity types and their weighted criteria in `config/taxonomy.yaml`:
* **Studio Residency:** Lead with Fill Rate and 30-day Retention.
* **Corporate Wellness:** Lead with Stress Reduction Delta and Recommend Rate.
* **Community Organization:** Lead with "Felt Welcomed" Belonging Score and Sliding-Scale Policy.
* **School:** Lead with Background Check, Liability Insurance, and Youth Group Handling.
* **Hotel / Resort:** Lead with Early Morning Availability and 5-Star Guest Reviews.
* **Festival:** Lead with Peak Crowd Attendance and Energy Scores.
* **Private Event:** Lead with Host Repeat Bookings and Customizable Rate Cards.
